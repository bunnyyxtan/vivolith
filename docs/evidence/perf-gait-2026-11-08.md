# Movement and frame-time check, 2026-11-08

Kavi glitch (lying on his back while walking): walk/run clips from `player.glb` were copied as local bone
rotations onto Kavi's Tripo-derived rig, whose bone rests differ by up to 180 degrees. Fixed by retargeting
through world-space rotations (`retargetClip` in `src/character.ts`). Evidence: `gait-walk.jpg`,
`gait-run.jpg`, `gait-walk-back.jpg`, `gait-walk-front.jpg`, `gait-run-back.jpg`, `gait-jump-stop.jpg`.

Frame time (dev build, this PC, 1249x846 canvas, step + render with gl.finish, 400 frames running and turning
down the avenue): median 6.4 ms, p95 8.7 ms, p99 10.3 ms, max 11.3 ms, 0 frames over 16.7 ms.
Render alone at 1920x1080: 5.6 ms median. Simulation step: 1.2 ms average, 4 ms max. Draw calls: 980.
Measured with the Browser pane hidden (no rAF), so rAF pacing itself was not measured.
